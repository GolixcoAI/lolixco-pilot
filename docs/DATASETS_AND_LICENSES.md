## 2. Dataset Licences and Provenance

### 2.1 Synthetic Training Fixtures (`training/data/manifest.json`)
- **Composition:** 7 structural JSON samples defining multimodal video conversation schemas, positive action labels, and negative distractors.
- **Provenance:** Synthetically generated by the engineering team to validate the Axolotl QLoRA training harness without ingesting, storing, or redistributing third-party copyrighted anime frames.
- **Licence:** MIT Licence (bundled with repository).

### 2.2 Local Evaluation Assets
- **Gojo Satoru Demonstration Clip (`gojo_hollow_purple_edit.mp4`):**
  - **Purpose:** Used strictly for non-commercial, offline functional evaluation and local browser acceptance testing.
  - **Exclusion from Repository:** In strict compliance with copyright standards, raw anime footage, extracted contact sheets, and keyframe stills are completely excluded from the Git repository, deployment packages, and public S3 buckets.
  - **Fair Use Notice:** This evaluation asset is referenced purely for transformational, educational, and benchmarking purposes within the hackathon context.
